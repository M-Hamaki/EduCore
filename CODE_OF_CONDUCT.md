# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in the EduCore community are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, threats, and sharing private or school data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the security contact or a private GitHub channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, demonstrations, and other project spaces.
